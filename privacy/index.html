PIKO – The Pinoy Game: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026
Developer: Kel Escueta
Contact: brandindigitalads@gmail.com

PIKO – The Pinoy Game ("PIKO", "the game", "we") is a casual game inspired by piko, the traditional Filipino street game. We built PIKO to be played without an account and with as little data as possible. This policy explains what information the game uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • You don't need an account. No email, password, phone number, real name or other personal details are ever requested.
  • Your progress stays on your device. We do not run any servers and we do not receive your game data.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. PIKO contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Leaderboards are optional. If you choose to use Apple Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android), your score is shared with that service so you can be ranked. Apple and Google operate those services under their own privacy policies.

Information stored on your device

PIKO saves the following information only on your device, so the game can remember your progress between sessions:

InformationWhy
Levels unlocked, and for each level you completed: best score, best accuracy, best time and number of completionsTo keep your progress, show your stars and best scores, and unlock the next level
Settings: music on/off, sound effects on/off, vibration on/offTo remember your preferences
A random anonymous player ID created on your deviceTo identify "this player" on this device without any account. It contains no personal information and is not sent anywhere

This information is not sent to us or to any third party. It is removed when you choose Settings → Reset progress (progress only) or when you uninstall PIKO (everything).

Apple Game Center and Google Play Games (optional)

PIKO offers an all-time leaderboard through Apple Game Center on iOS and
Google Play Games Services on Android. Using it is optional: the whole game is playable without signing in.

If you are signed in to Game Center or Play Games:

  • PIKO sends your total score (the sum of your best scores) to that service's leaderboard, and asks the service for your leaderboard rank so the home screen can show it (for example, "You're in 2nd place!").
  • PIKO displays your Game Center / Play Games nickname in Settings. The nickname is used only while the game is open and is not saved by PIKO.
  • PIKO never reads, shows or stores your Game Center or Play Games player identifiers.
  • Your score, nickname and rank are visible to other players on that leaderboard, as provided by Apple or Google.

Sign-in, leaderboards and any data those services collect are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can sign out of, or limit, Game Center in your iPhone's Settings → Game Center, and Play Games in the Google Play Games settings on Android.

Permissions

PIKO does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files. On Android, the game uses the standard permissions for vibration (for touch feedback), audio settings (for game sound) and internet access (only for the optional Play Games leaderboard).

Children

PIKO is designed to be enjoyed by players of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The game works fully without any account. Game Center and Google Play Games are provided by Apple and Google, which offer their own parental controls for children's accounts (for example, Screen Time on iPhone and Family Link on Android).

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or share personal information. Apart from the optional leaderboard score described above, PIKO does not send information off your device.

Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: delete your progress with Settings → Reset progress, or delete everything by uninstalling PIKO.
  • Leaderboards: scores you submitted are stored by Apple or Google. You can manage your Game Center or Play Games account and its data through Apple or Google. If you need help removing a score, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If PIKO changes how it uses information, we will update this policy and its effective date, and, where required, ask for your consent before the change applies.
