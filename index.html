THE PINOY GAME

PIKO – The Pinoy Game

Remember playing piko on the street after school? PIKO brings the classic Filipino hopscotch to your phone — chalk boxes, tsinelas and all. The street moves toward you; when a chalk box reaches your feet, tap it to land!

How to play

  • One box with a footprint: tap it with ONE finger.
  • Two boxes joined by “=”: tap BOTH at the same time with two fingers.
  • Numbered boxes: tap 1, then 2, then 3 — in order.
  • Land every row and hop all the way to LANGIT to clear the level. New to the game? Open Guide → Try it! Practice in the app.

Questions

Do I need an account?
No. Just tap Play. There are no sign-ups, ads or in-app purchases.

Why don't I see my ranking on the home screen?
Rankings use Apple Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android) and are optional. Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings → Game Center (or to Google Play Games on Android), then finish a level. New scores can take a few minutes to appear. The line under Player in PIKO's Settings tells you what's missing.

How do I start over?
In PIKO, open Settings → Reset progress.

The two-finger boxes don't register.
Touch both boxes at the same moment, with two separate fingers, when the boxes reach your feet. Try the practice course in the Guide — it has extra-generous timing.
